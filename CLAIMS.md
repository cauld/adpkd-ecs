Locked language for the manuscript. The Scribe may not exceed this.

## We will claim (if tests pass)

1. In GSE185948, *CNR1* is detectable in a **frozen** proximal-tubule / failed-repair epithelial state defined **without** *CNR1* as a marker (Gate C).
2. **If A1 ran and passed:** within that PT state, *CNR1* is higher in ADPKD than in control kidneys on the pre-specified test.
3. **If only A2 was eligible and passed:** in ADPKD samples, *CNR1* is higher in that PT state than in other renal epithelium — a map claim, **not** disease-specific induction.
4. States were not named with *CNR1*, and an unstratified whole-kidney mean was not used to pass Gate A (Gate B wall).
5. **If A1 passed and Gate S passed:** under the same freeze, the within-PT *CNR1* ADPKD-vs-control upshift is **not** recapitulated as DKD-vs-control in GSE195460 — a **specificity** claim, not a proof that CB1 causes cysts.
6. **If A1 passed and Gate S failed:** do **not** claim ADPKD-specific *CNR1* induction from these atlases.

A **negative** on A1/A2 is still a complete v1 if Gate C holds: the briefing’s PT-restricted, disease-induced *CNR1* story is reported as not supported in the ADPKD atlas. Gate S is then **not** run as a rescue.

## We will not claim

- Patients should start or stop cannabis, CBD, THCV, rimonabant, or peripheral CB1 antagonists.
- CB1 **is** the driver of cyst growth, fibrosis, or ESKD.
- THCV, JD5037, AM1241, or MQ1 treat ADPKD.
- CBD is contraindicated or indicated with tolvaptan (study 2 may map **uncertainty**; it still may not convert this paper into a DDI claim).
- Synthetic cannabinoid AKI epidemiology as a confirmatory result of this atlas.
- A docking pose, ΔG cutoff, or CYP inhibition prediction.
- GSE7869 or PDB files as confirmatory for v1.
- A2 pass as proof of ADPKD-specific induction.
- Gate S pass as proof that ECS dysregulation is unique to ADPKD among all CKD.
- A Gate A or Gate S **fail** as proof that *CNR1* is unchanged (public n is a large-effect test only).

## Framing that stays even if Gate A is null

A careful public-atlas audit of the published *CNR1* map is still the paper, reported negative if Gate A fails.
