# QUESTION

**One sentence.** Using FDA labels, trial registries, KDIGO, and published human evidence, what can be stated—and what must remain **unknown**—about cannabis, CBD, synthetic cannabinoids, and CB1-targeting drugs in people with ADPKD, including co-use with tolvaptan?

**Why this question.** People with ADPKD use cannabis/CBD for pain; KDIGO already tells clinicians to counsel about cannabis; there is no ADPKD cannabinoid RCT. The briefing overstated CBD as a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor versus Jynarque. A sealed map can bound that DDI, keep plant cannabis distinct from CB1 antagonists, and forbid treatment claims. It cannot find a cyst drug.

**What this is not.** A GEO *CNR1* test (that is study 1 at repo root). A docking or CYP **model**. A recommendation to start or stop cannabis. A documented CBD–tolvaptan PK trial (none found at Specify; confirm at run).

**Success for v1.** A pre-specified source list and search strings, a four-class exposure taxonomy, a label-bounded statement on CBD–tolvaptan (documented vs analog vs unknown), and a claims ceiling that survives a null trial search. A finding that the pair is **unstudied** is a complete paper.
