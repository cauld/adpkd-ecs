OSF FIELD: Description
OSF PROMPT: Write a detailed description of your research project, including its purpose and any expected outcomes. This should give readers a comprehensive overview of your work.
ANSWER:
This registration is study 2 of the ADPKD-ECS program (https://github.com/cauld/adpkd-ecs): a pre-specified evidence map of cannabis, CBD, synthetic cannabinoids, and CB1-targeting drugs in people with ADPKD, including co-use with tolvaptan. It uses existing public sources (FDA/DailyMed labels, ClinicalTrials.gov, KDIGO 2025, and published human evidence). It is not a GEO CNR1 analysis (that is a separate registration, study 1), not a docking or CYP model, and not a recommendation to start or stop cannabis.

Purpose. People with ADPKD use cannabis/CBD for pain; KDIGO already tells clinicians to counsel about cannabis; there is no ADPKD cannabinoid RCT identified at protocol freeze. Informal briefing text overstated CBD as a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor versus Jynarque (tolvaptan). This study locks a four-class exposure taxonomy, a frozen ClinicalTrials.gov query, label-bounded facts for Jynarque and Epidiolex, and a classification of CBD–tolvaptan co-administration as documented pair PK, labeled interaction, analog-only, or unstudied. Protocol freeze: git SHA db44b3086ae8c4d640dc40f44945bcef27ffe6bc.

Expected outcomes. Gate T: plant cannabis, CBD (OTC vs prescription when sources allow), synthetic cannabinoids, and CB1-targeting drugs remain separate classes. Gate R: the frozen registry query returns zero ADPKD/PKD studies with a cannabis/cannabinoid intervention, or an honest NCT list (no silent dropping). Gate L: Jynarque and Epidiolex labels are quoted for CYP3A / hepatotoxicity, Epidiolex midazolam and P-gp statements, and whether each names the other product. Gate U: CBD–tolvaptan is classified unstudied unless a dedicated pair PK study or a labeled interaction is found at run time; analog data (midazolam, everolimus, tacrolimus, grapefruit) cannot be rewritten as “contraindicated” or “safe.” An empty trial search and an unstudied pair are complete findings. The paper is an uncertainty map with a claims ceiling, not a demonstration that cannabis is safe or effective in ADPKD.

ITEM 3. Research questions
ANSWER:
Using FDA labels, trial registries, KDIGO, and published human evidence, what can be stated—and what must remain unknown—about cannabis, CBD, synthetic cannabinoids, and CB1-targeting drugs in people with ADPKD, including co-use with tolvaptan?
RQ-T. Can the four exposure classes be kept separate (plant cannabis; CBD OTC vs prescription when sources allow; synthetic cannabinoids; CB1-targeting drugs)?
RQ-R. Does ClinicalTrials.gov contain any study meeting the frozen ADPKD/PKD condition query AND a cannabis/cannabinoid intervention query?
RQ-L. What do the frozen Jynarque and Epidiolex labels actually say about CYP3A, hepatotoxicity, midazolam/P-gp, and whether each names the other product?
RQ-U. Is CBD–tolvaptan co-administration documented pair PK, a labeled interaction, analog-only, or unstudied?

ITEM 4. Hypotheses
ANSWER:
This is a structured evidence map, not NHST. “Hypotheses” are pre-specified classification rules (KILL.md). Human marks pass/fail.

Named confounds:
  (1) Collapsed exposure — plant cannabis, CBD, synthetics, and CB1 drugs treated as one intervention.
  (2) Label laundering — a theoretical CYP3A4 story reported as a documented CBD–tolvaptan interaction, or “no CYP3A4 effect on midazolam” reported as proof the pair is safe.

Gate T (taxonomy):
  Pass: results have at least four rows/sections: (i) plant cannabis, (ii) CBD (OTC vs prescription distinguished if sources allow), (iii) synthetic cannabinoids, (iv) CB1-targeting drugs (antagonist / inverse agonist / antibody as labeled in sources).
  Fail: one pooled “cannabinoid” recommendation.

Gate R (registry):
  Data: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, frozen query in PROTOCOL.md, run once at Unit 01, UTC date recorded.
  Pass: query string, date, and either zero studies meeting the ADPKD + cannabinoid intervention rule, OR a complete list of hits with NCT IDs (no silent dropping). Borderline hits listed with include/exclude reason.
  Fail: query not frozen, or hits omitted.
  A hit counts if the study lists an ADPKD/PKD condition AND a cannabis/cannabinoid intervention (not merely a mention in eligibility prose without intervention).
  Prediction at freeze: Specify-era search found no ADPKD cannabinoid RCT; Unit 01 must re-run. Empty search is a complete finding (CLAIMS.md).

Gate L (labels):
  Pass: Jynarque (tolvaptan) and Epidiolex (cannabidiol) labels cited (DailyMed setids below). Write-up states: (a) whether each names the other drug; (b) tolvaptan CYP3A / boxed liver warning as on the label; (c) Epidiolex midazolam and P-gp statements as on the label.
  Fail: labels not opened, or briefing text substituted for the label.

Gate U (DDI class):
  Allowed classes for CBD + tolvaptan: documented pair PK; labeled interaction; unstudied (default if neither of the above); analog only (midazolam, everolimus, tacrolimus, grapefruit as bounds, not as the pair).
  Pass: pair classified unstudied unless a dedicated PK/DDI study of that pair is found and cited, or a labeled contraindication / named interaction exists.
  Fail: scribe writes contraindicated or safe for the pair without a pair-specific study or a labeled contraindication.
  Analogs cannot convert U into “contraindicated” or “safe.”

Decide: T+R+L+U pass = complete v1 map. T fail = rewrite, do not seal a pooled-cannabinoid paper. U fail = not confirmatory. R or L fail = incomplete; amend or stop.
