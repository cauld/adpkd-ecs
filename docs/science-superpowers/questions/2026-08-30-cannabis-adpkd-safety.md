# Cannabis, CBD, and CB1 drugs in ADPKD — evidence map

**Research question:** Using FDA labels, trial registries, KDIGO, and published human evidence, what can be stated—and what must remain unknown—about cannabis, CBD, synthetic cannabinoids, and CB1-targeting drugs in people with ADPKD, including co-use with tolvaptan?

**Background / motivation:** Pain is common in ADPKD; some patients use cannabis/CBD. KDIGO 2025 addresses counseling, not a cannabinoid RCT. The source briefing overstated CBD–CYP3A4–tolvaptan as a documented contraindication. Study 1 (GEO *CNR1*) cannot answer this. Separate seal.

**Hypotheses:**
- H0: A dedicated CBD–tolvaptan PK study or labeled pair interaction exists (to be checked, not assumed).
- H1 (expected at Specify): the pair is **unstudied**; trial registry has no ADPKD cannabinoid intervention study; labels do not name each other.

**Population & unit of analysis:** Published human evidence and regulatory labels. Unit is the source (trial, label, guideline, paper), not a nucleus.

**Key variables:** Exposure class (four-way taxonomy); presence/absence of ADPKD trial; label interaction text; DDI class (documented pair / labeled / unstudied / analog only).

**What counts as an answer:** Human-marked gates in `studies/cannabis-safety/KILL.md`.

**Scope & exclusions:** No GEO counts, no docking, no FAERS ROR, no clinical prescription.

**Open questions for prior-work survey:** Exact DailyMed setids; KDIGO section numbers for cannabis practice points; whether any non-CT.gov registry holds an ADPKD CBD trial.

**Canonical SEAL copies:** `studies/cannabis-safety/QUESTION.md` and siblings. If this file and those disagree after Seal, SEAL wins.
