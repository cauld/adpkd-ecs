# QUESTION

**One sentence.** After freezing proximal-tubule / failed-repair epithelium **without** *CNR1* as a marker, is *CNR1* higher in ADPKD than in control **inside that state** in GSE185948 (after a composition check), and is that within-PT induction **absent** in diabetic kidney disease under the same freeze (GSE195460)?

**Why this question.** A 2026 paper and the source briefing treat PT-restricted, ADPKD-specific *CNR1* upregulation as the license for later CB1-antagonist chemistry. Their snRNA-seq primary statistic averaged *CNR1* across all cell types per sample. This study is a registered audit of that map: frozen states, within-PT disease contrast, composition wall, and a locked DKD specificity contrast. If the map fails, docking stays parked. If it passes, it is still not a treatment claim.

**What this is not.** A test of whether patients should take cannabis, CBD, THCV, or rimonabant. A CBD–tolvaptan interaction study (that is [`studies/cannabis-safety/`](studies/cannabis-safety/)). A docking campaign. A claim that CB1 antagonism treats ADPKD.

**Success for v1 (solo computational paper).** Frozen cell-state definitions that do not use *CNR1* as a marker, on **both** accessions, committed before any *CNR1* differential; plus pre-specified tests that *CNR1* either **is** or **is not** induced in ADPKD PT, **is** or **is not** explained by cluster abundance alone, and **is** or **is not** recapitulated in DKD PT. A clean negative is a complete v1. Chemistry (study 3) waits on Decide.
