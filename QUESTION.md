# QUESTION

**One sentence.** In public adult ADPKD snRNA-seq (GSE185948), is *CNR1* expression enriched in a **frozen** proximal-tubule / failed-repair epithelial state relative to other renal epithelium, after a composition check that would kill a whole-kidney-only story?

**Why this question.** A briefing argues that ADPKD is a metabolic–fibrotic disease with ECS collapse: *CNR1* up in failed-repair proximal tubule, ligands down, and CB1 as a maladaptive driver rather than a cAMP brake. That claim is the gate for every later cannabinoid-therapeutic idea (peripheral CB1 block, CB2 agonism, THCV analogs, MQ1 combinations). If *CNR1* is not cell-type-restricted in the named public atlas, the therapeutic story should shrink or stop before docking or mouse work.

**What this is not.** A test of whether patients should take cannabis, CBD, THCV, or rimonabant. A docking campaign. A V2R / tolvaptan / MQ1 trial. A claim that CB1 antagonism treats ADPKD.

**Success for v1 (solo computational paper).** A **frozen** cell-state definition that does not use *CNR1* as a marker, plus a pre-specified test that *CNR1* either **is** or **is not** restricted to that state (and is not explained by cluster abundance alone). A clean negative is a complete v1: later pathways stay in `EXPLORE.md`.
